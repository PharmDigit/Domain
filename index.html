About the Company

PharmDigit Technologies Inc is a Texas-based software innovation firm specializing in precision-driven digital solutions for the life sciences. The company is founded and developed by professionals with expertise spanning clinical pharmacy, biomedical research, and software engineering.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between complex pharmaceutical theory and real-world application by delivering accurate, reliable, and intuitive computational tools. We focus on transforming traditionally manual, error-prone calculations and workflows into streamlined, technology-enabled solutions.

PharmDigit Technologies develops mobile applications designed to:

  • Enhance pharmacy and health science education through structured computational training
  • Support clinical and academic calculations with validated formula-based tools
  • Assist laboratory workflows including buffer preparation, dilution calculations, and protocol standardization
  • Improve patient-centered outcomes through medication management, adherence support, and cost tracking

Our products are designed with an emphasis on precision, reproducibility, and practical usability across educational, clinical, and research environments.

Products

Pill Manager

Pill Manager is a patient-centered medication management application designed to support adherence, organization, and financial awareness. The platform enables users to track medication schedules, monitor refill history, record medication-related costs, and manage insurance-related expenditures. Additional features include reminder systems, personal health notes, and integration of cost-saving tools such as coupon references.

PharmCal – Clinical & Research Calculator Suite

PharmCal is a comprehensive pharmacy and life sciences calculation platform designed for students, educators, and healthcare professionals. The application integrates approximately 70 built-in calculators and a structured learning system with over 2,000 practice problems.

The platform covers 18 specialized calculation domains:

  • Dosage Calculations
  • Basic Pharmacokinetics (PK)
  • Pediatric Calculations
  • Apothecary and Avoirdupois Systems
  • Clinical Pharmacy Calculations
  • Community Pharmacy Calculations
  • Concentration and Dilution
  • Density and Specific Gravity
  • Electrolyte Calculations
  • Isotonicity and Buffer Systems
  • Medical Cost Calculations
  • Parenteral and Intravenous (IV) Calculations
  • Radiopharmaceutical Calculations
  • Reducing and Enlarging Formulations
  • Special Patient Populations
  • Unit Activity and Potency Calculations
  • Veterinary Calculations
  • Chemotherapy Calculations

In addition to core computational tools, PharmCal includes challenge-based problem sets, pre-examinations, and comprehensive final exams designed to reinforce conceptual understanding, computational accuracy, and exam readiness.

PharmLab

PharmLab is a laboratory-focused computational and workflow support tool designed for research and academic environments. The application assists with buffer preparation, concentration and dilution calculations, and implementation of standardized laboratory protocols. Additional functionality includes experimental planning support and sample tracking to enhance reproducibility and efficiency.
